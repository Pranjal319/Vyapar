# VYAPAR: SIH Presentation Demo Script 📜🎙️

**Target Duration**: 5–7 Minutes  
**Primary Objective**: Demonstrate to SIH judges how VYAPAR transforms static historical trade networks into an engaging 2D educational game.

---

## ⏱️ Timeline & Presentation Flow

### 0:00 – 0:45 | Introduction & Problem Statement
* **Speaker**: "Good morning/afternoon, esteemed judges. Traditional history education often presents India's ancient trade networks as static, isolated facts in textbooks. Students memorize lists of goods without understanding *why* trade routes existed or *how* geography, regional demand, and cultural exchange interacted."
* **Action**: Display VYAPAR Home Screen. Highlight the **"WHY VYAPAR?"** and **"WHAT THE PLAYER LEARNS"** sections.

### 0:45 – 1:30 | Solution & Demo Mode Launch
* **Speaker**: "VYAPAR solves this by turning history into an interactive 2D RPG trading adventure. Let us launch our dedicated 5-minute SIH Presentation Demo."
* **Action**: Click `[ 🏆 PLAY SIH DEMO ]`. Game initializes instantly in Ujjain with 500 Mudras.

### 1:30 – 2:45 | Local 2D Exploration & Educational Challenge
* **Speaker**: "The player walks around the 2D world using WASD controls. In Ujjain—the ancient crossroads of India's Dakshinapatha highway—we approach the Elder Merchant Narrator."
* **Action**: Walk player to Narrator (`[ E ] TALK`). Read dialogue and trigger historical question. Select correct answer.
* **Speaker**: "Answering correctly earns +10 Knowledge points and discovers a location insight logged into the player's Knowledge Journal."

### 2:45 – 4:00 | Market Economy & Commodity Trading
* **Speaker**: "Next, we approach the Ujjain Textile Stall. Each city has dynamic regional supply and demand. Ujjain produces fine textiles at a low cost of 45 Mudras per unit. We buy 4 units."
* **Action**: Open Market (`[ E ] Trade`). Purchase 4 units of Textiles. Show HUD cargo indicator updating (`📦 4/10`).

### 4:00 – 5:15 | Strategic India Trade Map & Caravan Journey
* **Speaker**: "We press **M** to open the Strategic India Trade Map. We select our destination: Bharuch, the western sea port where textile demand is high."
* **Action**: Open Map (**M**), select Bharuch port route, and start caravan travel.
* **Speaker**: "During caravan travel, historical events occur—such as encountering monsoon rains or dust storms—requiring decision-making under risk."

### 5:15 – 6:30 | Destination Arrival & Trade Result
* **Speaker**: "Upon arriving at Bharuch, we enter the harbor market and sell our textiles for 85 Mudras per unit—generating a net profit of +160 Mudras!"
* **Action**: Sell Textiles at Bharuch Port Market. The **VYAPAR DEMO COMPLETE** summary overlay opens.

### 6:30 – 7:00 | Conclusion & Impact
* **Speaker**: "In just 5 minutes, we explored a historical city, answered an educational question, bought regional goods, navigated trade geography, handled travel events, and made a profitable trade. VYAPAR makes India's history alive, memorable, and interactive. Thank you!"
